1. Executive Summary & Vision

FLARE (Fire & Life-Safety Analysis and Risk Explorer) is an interactive aerospace combustion research, spacecraft life-safety engineering, and scientific educational platform. Created by Team WUNDERBAO for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, FLARE bridges complex microgravity combustion data with an intuitive, visually stunning, and interactive user experience.

By coupling real-time analytical combustion models (D² Law droplet and solid-sphere equivalent dynamics) with 16 verified NASA flight investigations from the Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) database, FLARE enables aerospace researchers, engineers, university students, and K-8 classrooms to explore, compare, and understand the critical physics governing spacecraft fire behavior.

The Core Problem Solved: On Earth, gravity pulls cool air down and pushes hot combustion exhaust up, stretching flames into buoyant vertical plumes. In microgravity, this buoyant lift vanishes. Flames become spherical, blue diffusion shells fed exclusively by slow molecular motion or forced HVAC airflow. Because fire behaves so counter-intuitively in space, spacecraft fire detection, flammability standards, and suppression protocols require specialized understanding—yet this critical scientific data has historically been siloed inside dense academic repositories. FLARE unlocks this research for everyone.

2. Dual-Experience Architecture

FLARE serves two distinct audiences under a unified, accessible design language:

01 · Scientific Experience Researchers & Engineers

A high-data-ink research workstation designed for aerospace engineers, combustion researchers, university academics, and competition judges:

  • Interactive 3D Simulation Chamber: 5 diagnostic optical overlays (Flame Appearance, Temperature Map, Diffusion Gas Flow, Oxygen Concentration Zone, Soot/Smoke Yield).
  • Analytical Physics Telemetry: Real-time numerical tracking of burning rate (K), flame temperature (T_flame), aspect ratio (AR), and heat release rate (HRR).
  • Multi-Gravity Regimes: ISS Microgravity (0.001g), Lunar Habitat (0.16g), Martian Exploration (0.38g), Earth Reference (1.0g), and Centrifugal High-G (2.0g).
  • Scientific Comparison Workbench: Side-by-side benchmarking of flight datasets vs. analytical models with explicit compatibility indicators.
02 · FLARE Kids Universe Ages 7–13 & Classrooms

A museum-grade, bright, welcoming discovery environment designed for young students, families, and K-8 educators:

  • 5 Guided Science Worlds: Step-by-step visual storytelling explaining why fire is round in space, how oxygen feeds flames, and how astronauts stay safe.
  • Interactive 2D Sandbox (SpaceFlame): Safe experimentation with gravity toggles, oxygen levels (low/normal/high), and still vs. moving cabin air.
  • Sparky the Space Flame Guide: A warm, pedagogical AI companion using everyday analogies to make complex thermodynamics intuitive.
  • Ethical Educational Design: Zero gamification gimmicks, badges, or points—focused entirely on fostering authentic scientific curiosity.

3. Key Platform Capabilities & Innovation

A. Analytical D² Law Simulation Engine

The core simulation engine implements the classical combustion D² Law droplet and spherical solid-equivalent model:

D²(t) = D₀² - K_eff · t
The burning rate constant (K_eff) dynamically couples empirical oxygen-mole-fraction enhancements, ambient pressure corrections, and ventilation airflow enhancements via convective Sherwood/Nusselt numbers. This provides instantaneous, responsive telemetry without pretending to run a heavyweight 3D Navier-Stokes CFD solver.

B. Verified NASA Research Database (16 Investigations)

FLARE organizes 16 landmark orbital microgravity combustion investigations from the NASA Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) archive, complete with official investigation metadata, principal investigator credits, flight platforms, and deep links:

Mission / Code Flight Platform Combustion Focus & Scientific Objective
Saffire I–VI Uncrewed Cygnus Cargo Vehicles Large-scale spacecraft fire safety, material flammability (PMMA, Sibal cloth), and low-pressure exploration atmospheres (up to 34% O₂).
FLEX & FLEX-2 ISS Combustion Integrated Rack Droplet combustion, spherical diffusion flame dynamics, multi-component fuels, and low-temperature "cool-flame" kinetics.
BASS & BASS-II ISS Microgravity Science Glovebox Burning and suppression of solid materials in low-speed forced flows for exploration spacecraft fire extinguishment.
ACME ISS Combustion Integrated Rack Advanced Combustion via Microgravity Experiments (5 gas-fuel modular experiments focused on soot reduction and pollutant control).
SAME & SAME-R ISS Destiny Laboratory Module Spacecraft Fire Smoke Detector Characterization testing particle morphology and optical obscuration under zero-gravity conditions.

C. Context-Aware Autonomous AI Science Agent

Operating in both the Scientific Experience and Kids Universe, FLARE's AI Assistant is powered by a multi-turn autonomous engine:

  • Context Retention: Automatically observes the user's active chamber parameters (fuel type, gravity, O₂, pressure, ventilation).
  • Autonomous Tool Invocation: Executes real mathematical tools (run_combustion_simulation, compare_combustion_states, search_nasa_experiments, retrieve_research_library).
  • Honest State Reporting: Transparently badges live Gemini API mode vs. deterministic offline fallback (DEMO MODE), explicitly communicating scientific assumptions and model boundaries.

4. Universal Accessibility & Mobile Optimization

FLARE was built with an uncompromising commitment to universal design, complying with WCAG 2.2 Level AA:

Cross-Device Responsive Engine
  • 100% Zero Horizontal Overflow: Validated across 6 standardized viewports: 320px (compact phone), 375px (iPhone SE), 390px (iPhone 14), 412px (Android Pixel), 768px (iPad tablet), and 1440px (desktop).
  • Mobile Form Optimization: Automatic font clamping at 16px on mobile screens to prevent unwanted iOS Safari viewport auto-zooming.
  • Dynamic Viewport Support: Full 100dvh support on AI slide-over panels and .pb-safe safe-area padding for notched screens.
Synchronized Accessibility System
  • Proportional Typography Scaling: 4 scale profiles: Small (92%), Default (100%), Large (112%), and Extra Large (124%) applied to root rem units.
  • High Contrast & Focus Ring: Enforces 7:1+ contrast ratios with a high-visibility 3px gold ring (#fbbf24) on active keyboard controls.
  • Reduced Motion Engine: Freezes canvas starfields and decorative orbital SVG rotations to accommodate photosensitive or vestibular needs.
  • Portable Device Profile Sync: One-click generation of shareable base64 URL links (?a11y=...) to configure mobile devices instantly.

5. Space Agency Data Provenance & Technology Stack

NASA Data Sources Used
  • NASA Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI): psi.nasa.gov — Investigation metadata, test geometries, and combustion records.
  • NASA Technical Reports Server (NTRS): ntrs.nasa.gov — Apollo 1 / Apollo 13 incident reports and Mir fire analysis.
  • NASA Saffire Mission Documentation: Spacecraft Fire Safety Demonstrations conducted aboard uncrewed Cygnus spacecraft.
  • NASA Safety Standard: NASA-STD-6001B — Flammability, Offgassing, and Materials Compatibility.
Core Technology Stack
  • Framework & UI: Next.js 14 (App Router), React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion.
  • 3D Scientific Engine: Three.js, @react-three/fiber, @react-three/drei.
  • Artificial Intelligence: Gemini API with autonomous multi-turn tool calling and deterministic offline fallback.
  • Cross-Tab Sync: Native Web APIs (BroadcastChannel, localStorage).

6. Scientific Honesty & Model Limitations

Model Transparency Commitment: FLARE explicitly badges all outputs so users distinguish between measured flight data, literature benchmarks, and model estimates.
  • 0D/1D Analytical D² Law: FLARE's simulator calculates analytical droplet/solid-sphere equivalent kinetics and does not claim to run full 3D Navier-Stokes computational fluid dynamics (CFD).
  • Saffire Flight Safety Context: Saffire I–VI experiments were conducted aboard uncrewed Cygnus cargo vehicles following ISS departure prior to atmospheric reentry—never inside an occupied crew habitat.
  • Zero Authentication Gates: The entire platform is 100% public, free, and instantly accessible to anyone worldwide.
