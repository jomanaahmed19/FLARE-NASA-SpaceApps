# Impeccable Reference — `critique` Playbook

Use this playbook to conduct a scored heuristic UX and visual design critique of any FLARE interface.

## Critique Dimensions (Score 1–10 each)

1. **Brand & Aesthetic Authenticity**
   - Does the screen feel like a genuine aerospace combustion research instrument (for `/science`, `/research`, `/compare`) or an engaging, approachable space discovery lab (for `/kids`)?
   - Are generic AI clichés (random purple/indigo gradients, unmotivated glowing blobs, generic SaaS cards) completely absent?

2. **Visual Hierarchy & Scannability**
   - Is the primary focal point immediately clear within 2 seconds of landing on the screen?
   - Do secondary telemetry labels recede appropriately (`text-slate-400` / `text-slate-500`) so primary metrics (`text-white`, `text-flame-400`, `text-cyan-400`) stand out?

3. **Workflow Continuity**
   - Can a user move effortlessly from discovering a NASA experiment (`/research`) -> testing its parameters in the 3D Simulation Lab (`/science`) -> comparing it side-by-side (`/compare`) -> asking the AI Assistant for an explanation?

4. **Scientific Credibility & Honesty**
   - Are measured NASA values, literature references, and D² Law model outputs visually distinct at a glance?
   - Are model assumptions and compatibility warnings surfaced right where decisions or interpretations are made?

5. **Cognitive Load & Distillation (`distill` / `quieter`)**
   - Is every border, badge, and card earning its keep?
   - For the Kids Experience (`/kids`), is the copy simple, warm, concrete, and free of intimidating equations?
